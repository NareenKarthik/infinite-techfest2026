Turn-by-Turn Wayfinding Roadmap

Floor 2 Venues: ROOM214 (Paper & Mech Hack X), MECH LH20 (Project Xplore & Quiz), and MECH LH 21 (Poster Pro).

1

Start at Saraswati Statue (Ground Floor)

Assemble at the central entrance foyer.

2

Take Staircase-SW or Elevator up to Floor 2

Turn left and climb two flights of stairs (past Floor 1) to Floor 2.

3

For ROOM214 (Paper Presentation & Mech Hack X)

Turn right into the corridor, proceed towards the East classroom wing, and turn left. ROOM 214 is on your right.

4

For MECH LH20 & MECH LH 21

Proceed straight through the southern corridor past the Staff Room:
• MECH LH 21: POSTER PRO (Hall on your right)
• MECH LH20: PROJECT XPLORE & BRAIN BOLT QUIZ (Hall on your right)
