Turn-by-Turn Wayfinding Roadmap

Floor 2 Hosts: Circuit Debugging (LH15), Tech Escape Room (LH16), ECE Hackathon (LH17), and Project Expo (Lamda Lab).

1

Start at Saraswati Statue (Ground Floor)

Assemble at the central entrance foyer.

2

Take Staircase-SW or Lift to Floor 2

Turn left and take the main southwest stairwell up two flights past Floor 1 to Floor 2.

3

For Project Expo (LAMDA LAB)

Turn left along the west corridor and proceed north to Lab-4 (LAMDA LAB) on your left.

4

For LH15, LH16 & LH17

Proceed straight through the south corridor past Staff Room, and turn left into the East Wing:
• LH15: Circuit Debugging (First room on right)
• LH16: Tech Escape Room (Middle room on right)
• LH17: ECE Hackathon (Third room on right)
