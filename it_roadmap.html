⚡ Claude Prompt Battle Brief

  • Venue: Beta Lab (Floor 1).
  • System accounts & high-speed workstations provided.
  • Rounds include reasoning puzzles, few-shot prompting, and automated eval benchmarks.
  • Scoring is based on prompt precision, token efficiency, and response accuracy.

📄 Innopapers Presentation

  • Venue: M213 Smart Class Room (Floor 1).
  • 8 minutes presentation + 2 minutes judge Q&A.
  • Interactive Smart TV with HDMI and wireless casting.
  • Carry your PPT / PDF on a USB drive and keep 2 hard copies.
