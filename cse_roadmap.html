Turn-by-Turn Wayfinding Roadmap

Floor 4 Hosts: 1) FEATURE MINE in Room M421 and 6) AI Prompt Battle (Using Claude AI) in Room M422.

1

Start at Saraswati Statue (Ground Floor)

Assemble at the central entrance foyer facing the main registration reception.

2

Take Staircase-SW or Elevator up to Floor 4

Turn left and take the main Southwest stairwell or elevator all the way up past Floor 1, Floor 2, and Floor 3 directly to Floor 4.

3

For AI Prompt Battle (Using Claude AI) — Room M422

Step out into the South corridor and walk east past the Seminar Hall. Room M422 (Smart TV Class Room) is located directly along the south wall on your right!

4

For FEATURE MINE — Room M421

Continue slightly further east past Room M422 along the South corridor. Room M421 (M421 ROOM) is the adjacent room right on your right side!
