Turn-by-Turn Wayfinding Roadmap
1

Assembly at Saraswati Statue (Ground Floor)

All participants assemble at the central Saraswati Statue foyer right past the main registration gate.

2

Head Left to Staircase (Stair-SW / Lift)

Turn left and take the main southern staircase (Stair-SW) or the adjacent elevator.

3

Climb to Floor 3

Ascend past Floor 1 and Floor 2, and step out onto Floor 3.

4

Follow the West Corridor to North Wing

Walk straight along the corridor, turn right at the corner towards the North Drawing Hall wing.

5

Arrive at DH2 SMART TV CLASS ROOM

Walk past the Staff Room and DH1. Destination DH2 (Drawing Hall 2) is on your right. You have arrived!
