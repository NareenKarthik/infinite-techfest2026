Turn-by-Turn Wayfinding Roadmap

Floor 2 Hosts 4 Key Events: 1) Pro-Pitch & 4) Code Flex in DELTA LAB; 2) VizCraft & 5) Cognix in THETA LAB.

1

Start at Saraswati Statue (Ground Floor)

Assemble at the central entrance foyer facing the main registration desk.

2

Take Staircase-SW or Elevator to Floor 2

Turn left and ascend two flights via the main Southwest stairwell (or take the elevator) past Floor 1 directly up to Floor 2.

3

Turn Left into the West Computer Lab Corridor

Step onto Floor 2 and turn left immediately into the dedicated software and AI laboratory wing.

4

For THETA LAB (VizCraft & Cognix)

Walk north along the corridor past R&D Lab. THETA LAB (Lab 13) is on your left. You have arrived!

5

For DELTA LAB (Pro-Pitch & Code Flex)

Continue straight past Theta Lab and Epsilon Lab towards the north end of the west corridor. DELTA LAB (Smart Lab) is situated on your left!
